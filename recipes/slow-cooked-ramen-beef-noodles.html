Slow Cooked Ramen Beef Noodles Recipe

This recipe will teach how to make delicious slow cooked ramen noodles
This recipe will feature tender chuck and ramen noodles as the main characters and a savory sweet sauce as a rather important character.With only 5 minutes of prep this recipe is perfect for those long busy weeknights

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 to 3 pounds beef chuck roast
  • 1 cup chopped onion
  • 1 cup shredded carrots
  • 1/3 cup dark brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 8 cloves garlic
  • 2 1/2 teaspoons grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tablespoons rice vinegar or white vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper
  • 1 1/2 cups beef broth
  • 3 packages ramen noodles, spice packet discarded
  • 1/3 cup thinly sliced green onions, plus more for garnish
  • Sriracha and toasted sesame seeds for serving (optional)

Steps

  1. Place chuck roast into a slow cooker. Add onion, carrots, brown sugar, soy sauce, garlic cloves, ginger, vinegar, sesame oil, red pepper, and beef broth. Cover and cook on High for 5 hours or on Low for 8 hours.
  2. Uncover and remove the beef. Shred the beef into small pieces using 2 forks, discarding any fatty bits. Return meat to the slow cooker.
  3. Add ramen to the slow cooker, submerging the noodles into the liquid and breaking them apart slightly if necessary. Cover and cook on high until noodles are tender, about 10 minutes.
  4. Stir noodles to combine with the meat and stir in green onions. Serve, topped with Sriracha, sesame seeds and more green onions if desired.
